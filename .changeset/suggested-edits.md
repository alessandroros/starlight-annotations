Suggested edits. Select a few words and press **Suggest edit**: write the text as it should read, and why if you like. The note shows the change word by word, removed words in red and new ones in green, with **Copy new text** to apply it by hand. Its author can change it with **Edit**. The overview page filters them under **Type: Suggested edits**, notifications say "suggested an edit" with the new text, and an issue opened from one shows a `diff` block. In the notes file, the thread has `suggestion`, and its `body` may be empty.
